# Synthesis outline

## Opening (unlabelled, before heading 1)
Answer the query directly in one short paragraph: what has actually been
measured, what has not, and the single most important structural fact — that the
published figures disagree mainly because they divide by different denominators
against different thresholds, not because they describe different worlds.

## 1. Attrition and Survival Rates of Creators and Channels
Establishes that no platform-scale survival telemetry exists, names where it was
searched for, then reports the four real measurements with their different units
(person, campaign, account, rank-position) and the two unpaid-population
analogues that show what such a curve would look like.

## 2. Income Distribution Across Creators: Median vs Mean and the Living-Wage Share
The report's densest section. Establishes the mean-to-median skew across every
platform measured, then presents the living-wage share as a matrix over
denominator and threshold, with the non-comparability crosswalk stating which
rows may never be pooled, and the two opposing measured biases bounding the
answer in both directions.

## 3. Time from Starting to First Meaningful Income
Establishes that the quantity is malformed rather than merely unmeasured:
eligibility gates are behavioural thresholds not durations, payout is gated a
second time by a $100 floor, and elapsed tenure does not separate successful
channels from unsuccessful ones. Supplies the three-part reframing in place of a
number of months.

## 4. Documented Reasons Creators Give for Quitting
Opens by naming the population mismatch — every instrument samples creators who
have not quit — then reports the four burnout instruments accurately as what they
are, sets them against the models of actual continuation, and closes on the
documented absence including its two near-misses.

## 5. Geographic Scope: Which Findings Are US/Western-Only, and What Is Known for Southeast Asia
Marks which of the preceding findings rest on US, UK or Western samples, then
gives the four measured mechanisms of non-transfer (living-wage denominator, RPM
gap, income composition, borrowed authority) and the nine-country evidence
showing the region is not one condition, with the per-country verdicts including
documented absences.

## 6. Evidence Quality and Measurement Caveats
Sets the two provenance chains side by side under one standard and reports the
result, quantifies the recirculation problem, records the two fabricated sources
encountered, and closes on the state of official measurement infrastructure.
