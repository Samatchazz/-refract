### Domain notes

Sourcing order: academic APIs first (Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, arXiv cs.CY/econ.GN, SSRN, NBER) for platform-labour and creator-economy panel studies — this literature is real and citation-ranked, and web search returns mostly vendor marketing in its place. Then platform-disclosed and administrative data (YouTube/TikTok transparency and payout disclosures, Patreon/Substack public payout data, national statistical agencies, tax-authority self-employment data). Then gray literature for Southeast Asia specifically: ADB, World Bank, ILO, national statistics offices (BPS Indonesia, PSA Philippines, NSO Thailand), and regional platform reports — SEA quantitative evidence is thin on the clear web and lives here. Vendor surveys (Influencer Marketing Hub, NeoReach, Linktree, Adobe, Kajabi) MAY be cited but MUST be labelled as self-selected vendor surveys with sample size stated, and must never outrank telemetry or peer-reviewed panel data on the same quantity. Evidence norms for this topic: measured platform telemetry and administrative records > peer-reviewed longitudinal panels > cross-sectional academic surveys > self-selected vendor surveys > press-cited round numbers with no traceable methodology (exclude these last entirely). Survivorship bias is the dominant failure mode — any sample recruited from active creators systematically overstates income and understates attrition, and this must be flagged wherever it applies. Recency window: 2018-2026, with 2021-2026 preferred; monetization-threshold changes make older cohorts non-comparable.
