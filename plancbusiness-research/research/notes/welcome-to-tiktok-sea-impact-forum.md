For the first time, TikTok will host **“TikTok Southeast Asia Impact Forum 2023"** in Jakarta, Indonesia. In the forum, TikTok will also launch a report titled **"The TikTok Effect: Accelerating Southeast Asia's Businesses, Education, and Community"** which will highlight how creativity and joy have sparked growth in Southeast Asia - from increasing economic opportunities, broadening access to education and self-improvement to creating a stronger sense of belonging for everyone in the community.
The first-ever report from TikTok will be launched on **June 15, 2023** at the TikTok Southeast Asia Impact Forum 2023 in Jakarta, Indonesia.
