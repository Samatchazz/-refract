# How many YouTube channels are there in 2026?
[Written by Tim Queen.](https://www.linkedin.com/mynetwork/discovery-see-all/?usecase=PEOPLE_FOLLOW&followMember=iamtimqueen)
## What is the total number of YouTube channels?
YouTube has 2.6 billion monthly active users. That is about 1 in 3 people from the entire Earth's population of 7.9 billion people, making YouTube the largest video sharing site and streaming video platform worldwide.
**YouTube has 113.9 million YouTube channels.** Only 4.4% of the 2.6 billion active YouTube users created their own YouTube channel.
This article breaks down the total number of YouTube channels and shows you how many channels have more than 100, 1K, 10K, 100K, 1M, 10M, 50M, and 100M subscribers.
Updated March 26, 2022. 
## Data Sources & Method
YouTube has neither published the number of YouTube channels on its platform nor provided a breakdown of how many channels fall within specific subscriber ranges.
Due to the lack of accurate and up-to-date YouTube statistics, I manually researched all figures in this article using open-source intelligence methods and data from Google, Google Ads, YouTube, SEMrush, SocialBlade, Statista, GlobalWebIndex, and SimilarWeb.

## How many YouTubers have 10,000 subscribers?
Two million YouTube channels have at least 10,000 subs. That is 1.75% of all YouTube channels or about 1 in 57 channels.
Some of the many benefits of having 10,000 subscribers include access to YouTube Stories and the ability to add a merch shelf under your YouTube videos via Merchandising on YouTube.
You also have access to YouTube Spaces, although YouTube indicated that it would discontinue this program.
## How many YouTubers have 100,000 subscribers?
321,100 YouTube channels have at least 100,000 subscribers. That is 0.28% of all YouTube channels or about 1 in 350 channels.
At this stage, YouTube will assign your channel a personal [YouTube Partner Manager](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6361049). You can think of a Partner Manager as your own personal YouTube expert with insider knowledge that can help you grow your channel even faster.
You also become eligible for your first physical award, the Silver Creator Award, aka the Silver Play Button.
## How many YouTubers have 1 million subscribers?
32,300 YouTube channels have at least 1 million subscribers. That is 0.028% of all YouTube channels or about 1 in 3,600 channels.
Only very few YouTube channels ever reach one million subs.
Once you reach this milestone, YouTube will send you a Gold Creator Award, aka the Gold Play Button.

## How many YouTube channels is the average person subscribed to?
On average, YouTube users subscribe to more than 40 YouTube channels. 
But that does not mean that average YouTube users only consume videos from 40 channels.
[YouTube's algorithm](https://timqueen.com/youtube-algorithm-explained/) only looks at channel subscriptions as hints regarding what topics someone might be interested in. The YouTube recommendation algorithm will always recommend other videos based on a user's personal preferences.
Between 60-90% of watch time typically comes from non-subscribers.
You can read more about how YouTube recommends content in my [YouTube content cluster strategy](https://timqueen.com/youtube-content-cluster-strategy/) article.
## How many monetized YouTube channels are there? (i.e How many channels are in the YouTube Partner Program?)
In August 2021, YouTube announced that [there are 2 million creators in the YouTube Partner Program](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/innovations-for-2022-at-youtube/). 
That is especially interesting since at least 10.1 million YouTuber channels with more than 1,000 subscribers fulfill at least one of two criteria to join the YouTube Partner Program. Yet only one in five channels joins.
Two main reasons cause this. Many YouTube channels with more than 1,000 subs never qualify for the requirement of gaining 4,000 hours of watch time, or they are ineligible because of copyright and community guideline violations.

## How many YouTube Premium subscribers are there?
YouTube Premium is an additional revenue stream for creators to replace YouTube ads for ad-free video viewing. YouTube Premium membership fees are split between the creators a member watches based on a percentage of their total watch history and behavior that month.
On September 1, 2021, [YouTube announced that it had surpassed 50 million Music and Premium subscribers](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/50-million/).
