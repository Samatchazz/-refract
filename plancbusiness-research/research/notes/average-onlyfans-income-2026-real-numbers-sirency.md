Most 'average OnlyFans income' content cites $5,000+/month. The honest median is $145/mo. Here's the actual earnings distribution and what shapes the curve.
## Average OnlyFans Income Breakdown
### The honest median: $145/month
OnlyFans' own data plus industry research show median creator earnings around $145-180/month. This number includes inactive creators (registered but not posting). Active creators (posting weekly+) median sits around $1,000-1,500/month.
### Earnings distribution by percentile
Bottom 50%: under $145/mo. Middle 40%: $200-2,000/mo. Top 10%: $5,000-100,000+/mo. Top 1%: $30,000-1,000,000+/mo. Heavy concentration at top tier by design - 1% earns 70%+ of total creator revenue.
### Average income by creator stage
Months 1-3: $0-500 (solo) / $200-1,500 (agency-managed). Months 4-6: $200-1,500 / $1,000-5,000. Months 7-12: $500-4,000 / $3,000-15,000. Year 2+: highly variable based on niche and consistency.
### Average income by niche (per-subscriber LTV)
Trans, mature, mom, granny, gay, couples: $40-100/sub LTV (highest). Latina, fitness, e-girl: $25-50/sub. Generic 18-25: $15-30/sub. See [niche breakdown](https://www.sirency.com/onlyfans-niches).
### Average income by traffic source
Reddit-led traffic: highest revenue per acquisition cost. Twitter: highest LTV from organic conversation. Instagram: largest volume but lower per-subscriber. TikTok: bridge funnel but lowest direct conversion. Pre-existing audience (Twitch/YouTube): 5-15% conversion to OF subscriptions.
### Why the 'average' is misleading
Average mixes creators who post weekly with creators who registered and never posted. Use median for healthier benchmark. Active-creator median is more useful: ~$1,000-3,000/month for committed creators with niche-fit positioning.
