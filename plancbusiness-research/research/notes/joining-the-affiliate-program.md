## How to Become an Affiliate
To become a Twitch Affiliate, you must meet the following 4 requirements:
  * Reach 25 Followers
  * Stream for 4 Hours
  * Stream on 4 Different Days
  * Reach an Average of 3 Viewers (on 4 different days)


You must enable [Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](https://link.twitch.tv/Enabling2FA) on your account to begin Affiliate onboarding. This is required for all Affiliates and Partners. If you're having trouble setting up 2FA, see [Login Issues Guide](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/twitch-account-access-login-issues-guide).
**Streamers under 18:** If you're 13-17 years old, you may join the Affiliate Program with parental or guardian consent and supervision. Parents and guardians should review our [Guide for Parents and Educators](https://safety.twitch.tv/s/article/Guide-Parents-Educators) for information about monetization and minors.
All four achievements must be fully completed to be invited to the Affiliate program. Track progress towards your Affiliate Program invite via the [Achievements section](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/achievements) of your Creator Dashboard.
You will be invited to the Affiliate Program via email and Twitch notification. It can take a few days after your channel becomes eligible to receive an invite.
### How is "Average of 3 Viewers" counted?
The 3 viewer requirement means you need an average **3 concurrent viewers** (watching at the same time), not 3 views in total. Days below 3 average viewers don't count against you.
**Example:** If you stream 10 days in the last 30 days, and 4 of those days had 3+ average concurrent viewers, you meet this requirement. The other 6 days don't affect your eligibility.
### Why do my dashboard numbers look different from Achievements?
Your Creator Dashboard shows your **all-time stats** , while the Achievements section shows your progress **within the last 30 days**. This is why the numbers may not match. For Affiliate eligibility, only the last 30 days count.
### When will I receive my Affiliate invite?
You will be invited to the Affiliate Program via email and Twitch notification. After your channel becomes eligible, it typically takes **24-48 hours** to receive an invite. In some cases, it may take up to 72 hours.
### What if I met requirements but wasn't invited?
If you've met all 4 requirements but haven't received an invite yet:
  1. **Wait 24-48 hours** - Processing typically takes 1-2 days after you meet all requirements
  2. **Check your email** - Look for an invitation from Twitch to join the Affiliate program (check spam/junk folders)
  3. **Verify all 4 requirements** - Double-check your [Achievements Dashboard](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/achievements) to confirm all requirements show as complete
  4. **Confirm the 30-day window** - All 4 requirements must be met within the same 30-day period
  5. **Check your notifications** - You may have received a Twitch notification in addition to email
  6. **Contact support after 72 hours** - If it's been more than 3 days, [contact Twitch support](https://link.twitch.tv/Contact-Us)


### I deleted or can't find my Affiliate invite email. How do I access onboarding?
The Affiliate invite email is a notification only. You don't need the email to begin your Affiliate onboarding. You can access it directly from your [Creator Dashboard](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/monetization/revenue/payout-onboarding) at any time.
If you've met the Path to Affiliate requirements and don't see the option to begin onboarding in your dashboard, please ensure your email address is verified in your [account settings](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/security). If everything looks correct and you're still unable to access onboarding, [contact Twitch Support](https://link.twitch.tv/Contact-Us).
Got your invite? View our first-time Affiliate walkthrough guide: [Affiliate Onboarding Guide](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/affiliate-onboarding-guide)
## Why Should I Be An Affiliate?
The Twitch Affiliate program unlocks monetization tools and community features. Here are the top benefits:
  * **Subscriptions** - Viewers can subscribe to your channel for exclusive perks
  * **Bits (Cheering)** - Earn revenue when viewers cheer with Bits [(Learn more)](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/cheering-for-partners-affiliates)
  * **Ad Revenue** - Earn money from ads shown on your channel
  * **Channel Points** - Create custom rewards for your community [(Learn more)](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/channel-points-guide)
  * **Extended VOD Storage** - Keep your broadcasts for 14 days instead of 7


These are just a few highlights. For a complete comparison of Affiliate features vs. other creator tiers, see [About Monetization on Twitch](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/about-monetization-on-twitch#monetization-features).
Visit [Creator Camp](https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/paths/monetize-your-content/twitch-basics/) for tutorials on how to use these tools.
## Common Onboarding Issues
If you're experiencing issues during the Affiliate onboarding process, here are some quick troubleshooting tips:
### Stuck on the tax interview (Step 3)?
  * **Try restarting onboarding** - Many form issues are resolved by starting over. See [How to Restart Onboarding](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/affiliate-onboarding-guide#restart) for step-by-step instructions and important information about payout timing
  * **Try a different browser** - Use Chrome, Firefox, or Edge in private/incognito mode
  * **Disable browser extensions** - Ad blockers and privacy extensions can interfere with forms
  * **Clear your cache and cookies** - Then try the form again


If the form has trouble accepting your legal name with accents or special characters (é, ñ, ö, etc.), [contact Twitch support](https://link.twitch.tv/Contact-Us). You must use your legal name exactly as it appears on your tax documents - do not alter or change your name to match the form.
### Form won't submit or shows an error?
  * **Wait if it says "Processing"** - Tax interview processing typically takes 24-48 hours, up to 72 hours in some cases
  * **Check your internet connection** - A stable connection is required to submit forms
  * **Try again later** - Temporary system issues may resolve on their own


### Need more help?
For detailed step-by-step guidance and troubleshooting, see the [Affiliate Onboarding Guide](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/affiliate-onboarding-guide). If you're still stuck after trying these steps and waiting 72 hours, [contact Twitch support](https://link.twitch.tv/Contact-Us).
## Leaving the Affiliate Program
If you want to leave the Twitch Affiliate Program, you'll need to contact Twitch support to offboard. Here's what you need to know:
### How to offboard
To leave the Affiliate program, [contact Twitch support](https://link.twitch.tv/Contact-Us). Support will verify your account and confirm when offboarding is complete.
### What happens when you offboard?
  * **You lose access to monetization tools** - Subscriptions, Bits, and ad revenue will no longer be available
  * **Existing subscriptions will expire** - Your current subscribers will not be charged again
  * **You keep your earned revenue** - Any unpaid balance will still be paid out according to the normal payout schedule
  * **You can reapply later** - If you meet the requirements again in the future, you can rejoin the Affiliate program


### Rejoining the Affiliate Program
If you previously left the Affiliate program and want to rejoin, [contact Twitch support](https://link.twitch.tv/Contact-Us). Your Affiliate Achievements typically remain completed, so you may not need to re-meet the requirements.
