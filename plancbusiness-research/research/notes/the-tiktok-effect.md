The Creator Economy 
Connects and Empowers SEA
Businesses have seen the power of the new Creator 
Economy. They have turned to the digital world to 
connect with and serve this dynamic, global audience. 
Brands now collaborate with independent creators on 
short-form videos that deliver compelling, authentic 
stories and cultivate valuable brand affinity.
  
It’s a virtuous cycle. As brands welcomed collaboration 
and community-centric marketing, creators rode the 
wave by monetizing their content on digital platforms.
  
Both businesses and creators use TikTok’s unique 
recommendation system to reach target audiences. 
TikTok makes it easier to tell brand stories with user-
friendly content creation tools and resources.  
Additionally, initiatives such as “Grow with TikTok: 
Starter Lab” have provided hands-on, interactive 
education for small and medium-sized businesses in 
SEA to build expertise as advertisers, and grow their 
businesses on the platform. TikTok has partnered with 
local governments and industry groups across the 
region to foster the growth of small and medium-sized 
businesses, such as collaborations with Thailand’s Digital 
Economy Promotion Agency, Indonesia’s state-owned 
enterprise Pertamina and the Vietnam E-commerce 
Association. 
Meanwhile, established businesses in SEA have used the 
TikTok Creative Exchange2, TikTok Creator Marketplace3 
and TikTok Branded Mission4 offerings to connect 
advertisers, brands and users. 
The Creator Economy has set the stage for creators and 
liberated the resources they need to earn an income by 
sharing their authentic experiences with the world.
With lowered barriers to entry in digital marketing, SEA’s 
people and businesses are now empowered to embrace 
digital transformation in order to fully leverage the 
opportunities presented to them.

The sum total of all human knowledge may be a click away in this digital era, but education still requires guidance: 
students need teachers and teachers need supportive communities.  
More people can teach and reach new students, and even more students have access to learning resources, thanks 
to wide-reaching networks and mobile devices, and technologies that allow students and teachers to be connected.
Nowhere is this more evident than in SEA, where virtual schools and digital tools bridge multicultural and geographic 
divides to share and spread knowledge and help people explore new skills and interests.
In April 2022, TikTok Indonesia launched a youth-
focused education campaign, #SerunyaBelajar 
(#TheJoyofLearning). It showcased a wide variety of 
educational-based content focused on self-development 
and betterment. The local hashtags #SerunyaBelajar 
and #SamaSamaBelajar currently have more than 27.7 
billion and 104.7 billion views on TikTok, respectively. 
Users have submitted more than 871,000 videos and the 
LIVE sessions garnered more than 3.9 million viewers.
#TikTokUni – the platform’s first educational campaign 
in Thailand – launched in 2020 to drive informative 
content and inspire creators. The three-year campaign 
changed perceptions of TikTok from a dancing-and-
singing app, to an edutainment platform amongst users, 
creators, government stakeholders, and business and 
media partners. #TikTokUni had more than 203 billion 
views in Thailand in early 2023.
Singapore’s #LearningIsFun campaign shows how the 
appetite for educational content on TikTok continues 
to grow. Between February 2020 and January 2021, 
the number of educational videos on the platform 
increased by more than 38 times, and #LearningisFun 
videos received over 344 million views.
Vietnam’s #LearnOnTikTok hashtag started in June 
2020 and has grown into the biggest umbrella hashtag 
for all informative content on the platform. More than 
1,000 creators have contributed content leading to 
more than 157 billion video views in Vietnam.
In Malaysia, the education and learning community is 
rapidly expanding. #TikTokGuru is one of the most used 
hashtags with more than 1.1 million interactive videos. In 
addition, #TikTokGuru video views have grown from 14.3 
billion views in 2022 to 24.7 billion views as of early 2023.
Supplementing other popular education hashtags in 
Philippines, TikTok launched the #LearnItOnTikTok 
campaign in October 2022. Within six months, there 
were 2.8 million #LearnItOnTikTok educational videos 
racking up 6.5 billion video views.

Alone, Together During COVID-19
When the world closed up during the pandemic, people opened up online. 
On digital platforms like TikTok, those who felt under-represented, vulnerable and isolated found a sense of 
community. They found a place to share their stories, learn from one another and grow with the support of caring 
and like-minded people they would otherwise never get the chance to connect with.
It started with simple health education programs. For example, in Singapore, TikTok teamed up with the 
Ministry of Communications and Information (MCI) to make trusted information available about the nationwide 
vaccination program. MCI successfully reached close to 1 million people in Singapore, with more than 1,400 
videos created and 4 million video views. The program was instrumental in reaching youths who had questions 
about vaccine programs.
Regional campaigns included:
#KhmerVacc discouraged the spread of fake news by offering factual information about vaccines and 
by rallying well-known TikTok creators to promote vigilance against misinformation.
#LawanCovid19 supported Indonesia’s fight against COVID-19 with innovative games and created a 
community of healthcare professionals to engage and educate users on health and vaccine safety.
#VaccinateLaos reduced COVID-19 misinformation by guiding users to find fact-based information 
and official resources about vaccines.
#KitaJagaKita supported the country’s vaccination drive by encouraging public health experts and 
healthcare professionals to address TikTok users’ biggest questions and concerns in livestreams.
#Resbakuna helped users overcome vaccine hesitancy and fear by sharing real stories of people who 
had received the vaccine.
#IGotMyShotSG supported Singapore’s nationwide vaccination drive by having TikTok users’ questions 
about vaccines answered by healthcare professionals on TikTok LIVE.
#SongKhoe247 brought together news partners and healthcare professionals to offer a holistic 
perspective of how to stay healthy and overcome the pandemic.
Altogether, more than 700,000 educational TikTok 
videos were created to engage communities with 
factual information about vaccines. These videos 
received more than 8 billion views in the region.
