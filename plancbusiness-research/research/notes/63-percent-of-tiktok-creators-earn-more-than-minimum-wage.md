KOMPAS/PRIYOMBODOBUMN Minister Erick Thohir appeared briefly at the live sale of local perfume products which took place at the launch of Creator House at the Kota Tua Post Office, Jakarta, Wednesday (10/7/2024). Creator House is a collaboration between Tiktok Indonesia and PT Pos Indonesia by providing a number of free facilities that creators on Tiktok can use to create content and promote local products through the MSME Corner.
JAKARTA, KOMPAS — More than 8 million creators from Indonesia receive income from [Tiktok](https://www.kompas.id/label/tiktok?open_from=automate_body_url). Many of them earn a monthly income that is above the average minimum wage.
Director of Communications Tiktok Indonesia Anggini Setiawan said, based on internal data as of December 2023, there were more than 8 million creators from Indonesia who received income from Tiktok. Then, according to the research report "The TikTok Effect: Accelerating Southeast Asia's Businesses, Education and Community Report"which Tiktok released in June 2023, as many as 63 percent of Indonesian creators on Tiktok earn income above the minimum wage.
Tiktok stated its intention to become a company that supports micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs). The presence of creators on Tiktok has proven to help MSMEs businesses grow.
In the same research report, Tiktok found that SMEs in Southeast Asia surveyed recorded an almost 50 percent increase in revenue through selling their products and services on Tiktok. Four out of five businesses in Southeast Asia have shifted from offline to online marketing channels by using Tiktok.
"As much as 60 percent of promotional content in Indonesia, both short videos and _live_ videos, on Tiktok is actually used to support local products on the Shop | platform. Tokopedia (formerly [Tiktok Shop Indonesia](https://www.kompas.id/label/tiktok-shop-indonesia?open_from=automate_body_url))," said Anggini at the launch of TikTok | Just post! Creator House, Wednesday (10/7/2024), in Jakarta.

Currently, according to Anggini, every month, Tiktok application is visited by 125 million people in Indonesia. Meanwhile, the number of sellers, including MSMEs on Tokopedia and Shop | Tokopedia has reached more than 21 million.

Regarding TikTok, Pos Aja! Creator House, explained that this is a space to facilitate the creation of content for SME products on the TikTok platform. The space utilizes a building in the Old Town area of Jakarta, previously used as a post office.
Erika Richardo, one of the Tiktok creators from Indonesia, confirms that Tiktok is becoming an excellent platform for creativity and financial stability. Currently, she has more than 14 million followers on Tiktok.
He regularly creates original video content. Apart from that, Erika also often receives endorsements from advertisers. "So far there are no signs of _endorsement_ dropping. Endorsement offers continue to rise. "Tiktok releases innovative features that support the attraction of creators," he said.
Erika has been a creator on Tiktok for three years now. Despite this, she is unwilling to disclose how much income she has earned as a Tiktok creator. "It's always enough every month," she said.

FAKHRI FADLURROHMANA seller offers their merchandise online through Tiktok social media directly from their booth in Blok B Tanah Abang, Jakarta on Tuesday (13/6/2023). Online selling has become one of the strategies used by merchants to reach a wider consumer base. A merchant said that 40-50% of their earnings come from online selling. In a month, they can make a turnover of around Rp 50 million.
At the same event, Minister of State-Owned Enterprises (BUMN) Erick Thohir was also present. He said that the [digital economy](https://www.kompas.id/label/ekonomi-digital?open_from=automate_body_url) market potential in Indonesia in 2030 is estimated to reach IDR 4,500 trillion.
Such a huge market potential should be a magnet for Tiktok and other digital technology companies to invest more in Indonesia compared to other countries in the Southeast Asian region.
"I hope Tiktok will not become a stranger in Indonesia. Tiktok should invest more in Indonesia than other countries. "The potential for Indonesia's digital economy is quite large," said Erick.
