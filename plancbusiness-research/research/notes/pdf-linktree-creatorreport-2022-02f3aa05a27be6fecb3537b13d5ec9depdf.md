summary: 'PRIMARY SOURCE, SELF-SELECTED VENDOR SURVEY. The Linktree 2022 Creator Report
  PDF including its full methodology page, which most citations omit. Two incompatible
  methods are bolted together. Chapter 1 is a top-down desk estimate (Linktree Creator
  Economy Research, 2021) that produced the famous ~200 million creators figure by
  taking total active users of each social platform and bucketing them by follower
  count using Statista, Omnicore Agency and Global Media Insight data, explicitly
  excluding Douyin from the TikTok count. Chapters 2-3 are a survey of 9,576 Linktree
  users fielded in 2021 with a 95% completion rate and NO disclosed country, age or
  platform composition. Definition of creator is intent-based and income-free: if
  you produce and seek to monetize content you are a creator; individuals who use
  their influence, creativity, or skills to aggregate and monetize their audience.
  Income findings: 53% of all creators earned $100 or less per year; 66% identify
  as part-time; part-timers 68% under $1K and 3% over $50K; full-timers 46% under
  $1K and 12% over $50K. Time-to-first-income (the strongest datapoint here): among
  beginner creators with under 12 months experience, 59% had not monetized at all,
  35% had monetized but below a livable income (never numerically defined), and only
  6% had earned over $10K. Also: 67% never did a brand collaboration; 13% of full-timers
  extremely/consistently stressed vs 9% of part-timers; niche creators 7% over $100K
  vs 5% non-niche; no monotonic hours-to-income relationship (52% of $50K-100K earners
  spent under 10 hrs/week). CAVEATS: the follower-bucket model is internally implausible,
  showing only 23M creators at 0-1K followers but 139M at 1K-10K, inverting every
  measured follower pyramid, and the buckets sum to 207M against a 200M headline.
  The tenure chart label-to-value mapping (36/20/11/34 percent across 3+yr, 2-3yr,
  1-2yr, 1-12mo) is not recoverable from the extracted PDF text. The sample is drawn
  only from currently-active Linktree users, so it is survivorship-biased and its
  tenure distribution CANNOT be read as a survival curve.'

about linktree
The creator economy is an exciting space to be in. 
Linktree is making sense of this evolving economy 
by working with the people who lead it: the 
creators. We’re the go-to partner for creators, and 
thanks to this one-of-a-kind relationship, we can 
connect the rest of our industry to their world.
From partnerships with key players to unique 
interactive tools that can't be found anywhere else 
– everything we do and make exists to help 
creators thrive.
Methodology
Linktree Creator Economy Research (2021) 
Referenced internal Linktree data and external data from 
Statista, Omnicore Agency, Global Media Insight, and the 
social media platforms referenced. Total active users for 
each platform were broken down by the number of 
followers. TikTok analysis did not include Douyin.
Linktree Creator Survey (2021) 
Surveyed 9,576 Linktree users. The survey had a 95% 
completion rate. Response rates to individual questions 
varied due to open-ended questions and qualitative data. 
Percentages for all quantitative data were rounded to the 
nearest tenth or the nearest whole number as fit.
Linktree Creator Outreach (2021) 
Emailed select creators on Linktree with interview 
questions. Responses were tweaked for flow and length 
with respondents’ permission and approval. Follower 
counts noted are from early February 2022.
