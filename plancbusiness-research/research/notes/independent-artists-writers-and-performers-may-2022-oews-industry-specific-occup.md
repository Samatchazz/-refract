  (OEWS), May 2022 national industry-specific estimates for NAICS 711500 ''Independent
  Artists, Writers, and Performers''. This is the administrative payroll benchmark
  for the creator-adjacent occupation.


  Headline: All Occupations in NAICS 711500, employment 61,710 (employment RSE 0.1%),
  median hourly wage $24.58, mean hourly wage $35.75, annual mean wage $74,360 (mean-wage
  RSE 17.1%). Mean hourly is 1.45x median hourly — right-skewed. Within-industry detail:
  Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports and Media occupations 28,490 workers (46.17%
  of the industry), median $29.33/hr, mean $42.52/hr, annual mean $88,440. Artists
  and Related Workers 6,600, median $24.19/hr. Craft Artists 1,360, median $17.00/hr,
  annual mean $37,070. Fine Artists 3,670, median $24.53/hr. Special Effects Artists
  and Animators 800, median $39.82/hr. Entertainers and Performers 10,280, median
  $33.65/hr, annual mean $107,160. Disc Jockeys (except radio) 1,740, median $23.70/hr.
  Producers and Directors median $47.69/hr, annual mean $116,290.


  CRITICAL METHODOLOGICAL LIMIT: OEWS is collected ''from employers'', so it counts
  wage-and-salary jobs only and structurally EXCLUDES the self-employed — who are
  the majority of this occupation (NEA/ACS puts self-employment at 34-57% across artist
  occupations, and ACS-based Data USA counts 172,376 people in the same industry group
  versus BLS''s 61,710). The BLS figure therefore describes the small payrolled minority
  and cannot be read as the income of independent creators. Many detail rows are suppressed
  entirely for small cells. US-ONLY, May 2022 reference period.'
