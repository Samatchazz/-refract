The top three occupations in the Independent artists, writers, and performers Industry Group are [artists & related workers](https://datausa.io/profile/soc/271010), [writers & authors](https://datausa.io/profile/soc/273043), and [musicians and singers](https://datausa.io/profile/soc/272042). On average, full-time employees in the Independent artists, writers, and performers Industry Group work 44.5 hours per week and have an average annual salary of $85,559. Part-time employees in the same industry work 19.3 hours and earn an average annual salary of $31,594.
The locations with the highest concentration of employees in the Independent artists, writers, and performers Industry Group are [Los Angeles County (Central)--LA City (Central) PUMA, CA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/79500US0603775), [Los Angeles County (Central)--Burbank City PUMA, CA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/79500US0603720), and [Los Angeles County (Central)--LA City (East Central & Hollywood) PUMA, CA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/79500US0603732).
The workforce of Independent artists, writers, and performers Industry Group in 2024 was 172,376 people, with 43.8% woman, and 56.2% men.
In 2024, 70.8% of the workforce in the Independent artists, writers, and performers Industry Group was White (122,033 people), of which 44.1% were women and 55.9% men.
