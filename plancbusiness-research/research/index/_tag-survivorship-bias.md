- [[the-100b-creator-economy-revealed]] — The $100b+ "Creator Economy" Revealed — Money with Katie (Morning Brew) podcast transcript, June 21 2023, 10,209 words, US. LONG SOURCE -- flagged for source-analyst delegation. The single most methodologically self-aware commentary source in this batch: it does not merely report creator-income figures, it enumerates in advance why such figures are unreliable, then reports them anyway with the caveats attached.

Three named obstacles to measuring creator income are stated explicitly. (1) Survivorship bias, defined in the transcript as 'this occurs when only the strongest survive... tales of challenges and success are necessarily skewed by the fact that you are only hearing from the winners.' (2) No standard income definition -- creators earn via platform payouts, affiliate, direct sales, and pay-for-play in any combination, so 'it's not the same as asking, hey, what's the average salary for a software engineer with three years of experience.' (3) Gross-vs-net: agents, lawyers, taxes, healthcare and publicists are invisible in every headline earnings figure.

Crucially, the host reports a DATA ABSENCE as a finding: per ConvertKit research 'it was surprisingly difficult to find metrics about the overall amount of money sloshing around in the creator economy, and what percentage of influencers it's concentrated within.'

Also: first 1,000 subscribers in ~2 weeks and 100,000 within 2 years on the second channel, launched off an existing audience (a survivorship caveat the host names as 'what you're not seeing').
