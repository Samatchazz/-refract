LIVING-WAGE EVIDENCE: at time of research, 6 of the 7 Irish creators could NOT generate a livable income from YouTube and relied on full-time or freelance jobs — only one, a former production-house worker, could. All 9 Turkish participants did earn a sustainable full-time income, but 7 of the 9 worked inside a production house rather than independently, so the Turkish 'success' case is really institutional employment rather than solo creator viability. Sample sizes are too small and non-random for rate estimation — cite for mechanism and for the geographic RPM gap, not for prevalence. `evergreen`
